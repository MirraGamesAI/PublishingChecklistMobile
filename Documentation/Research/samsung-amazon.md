## Build facts

### Samsung Galaxy Store

- Package format: APK or AAB. From an AAB Galaxy Store generates one universal APK; Play Asset Delivery and Play Feature Delivery are not supported (https://developer.samsung.com/galaxy-store/faq.html). AAB uploads require Galaxy Store-managed signing (https://developer.samsung.com/galaxy-store/launch.html).
- Size: "2 GB+ is supported" for uploads (https://developer.samsung.com/galaxy-store/faq.html). No statement about OBB expansion files was found.
- Target API: targetSdkVersion 34 (Android 14) or higher for new apps and updates since 2024-11-01; China-only apps 30. Apps already on sale are not forced to update, but a target below 33 limits them to devices with the same or lower Android version (notice 0000008212). The FAQ still says ">=33" (stale). Apps with targetSdkVersion below 24 were set to Suspended on 2024-11-01. No Samsung notice requiring API 35 or 36 was found.
- Minimum API: no store minimum. Samsung IAP SDK 6.1.1+ requires API 23.
- 64-bit: at least one 64-bit binary required for new apps and updates since 2023-11-01 (notice 0000007772).
- 16 KB page size: mandatory for all Android binaries, including updates of existing apps; exempt: mainland-China-only apps, themes, stickers, watch apps, Instant Play 1.0. Enforcement start (2026-07-01) postponed; new date to be announced (notice 0000011451).
- Signing: APK is signed by the developer. AAB: own key uploaded or reused, or the Galaxy Store key. Different keys or package names prevent cross-store overwrites; Android 14 and later asks users to confirm an update from a different store (https://developer.samsung.com/galaxy-store/cross-store-updates.html).
- Google Android Developer Verification: package name plus signing certificate SHA-256 registered in Google's Android Developer Console. Brazil, Indonesia, Singapore, Thailand from 2026-09-30; global in 2027. Seller Portal shows "Installable" or "Not Installable" per binary and blocks unapproved binaries for those countries (notice 0000011990). For Galaxy Store-signed AABs, add the Galaxy Store key fingerprint from Seller Portal.
- Billing: Samsung IAP SDK recommended, not mandatory; third-party payment systems are supported by Samsung (FAQ). Current Android SDK 6.5.2 (2026-05-18); Unity plugin SamsungIAP_unity_plugin_v6.5.2.unitypackage (2026-07-01, Unity 2017.3.1 or later). IAP 4.x terminated 2026-07-31. Revenue share since 2025-05-15: 80/20, subscriptions 85/15, for sales through Samsung Checkout (https://developer.samsung.com/sdp/news/en/2025/03/13/new-revenue-share-model-for-galaxy-store). In-app products can be added only after a binary with Samsung IAP is registered (https://developer.samsung.com/iap/overview.html).
- Ads: no Samsung ad SDK or ad network requirement found; policy rules only (labels, close and skip buttons, age-appropriate content).
- Login: Galaxy Store Login exists (https://developer.samsung.com/galaxy-store/integrate.html lists it) but its page could not be read; no rule requiring it was found.
- Saves and game services: no Samsung cloud save or leaderboard service found.
- Review prompt: Galaxy Store Review Broadcast, Galaxy Store 4.5.22.7 or higher; eligible only if the user is logged in to a Samsung account, installed or updated the app from Galaxy Store and has not rated it in the last year.
- Age signals: Get Age Signals API (Galaxy Store 4.6.03.1+), live for Texas App Store Accountability Act; Louisiana and Utah postponed to 2027. Seller Portal has a "Minor Regulation Response" tab for material changes and exemptions (notice 0000011789).
- Optional: Galaxy Store Install Referrer library for attribution.
- Forbidden in the Galaxy build: Samsung IAP 4.x; test IAP operation modes in submitted binaries; debuggable builds; trial or beta binaries; self-update; shortcut creation; downloads of other apps.
- Permissions: minimum needed; for South Korea every permission is declared in Seller Portal with reasons.
- Review process notes: test account or self-test video in review comments; Data Safety tab; age rating is chosen in App Information; publication after review, on a date or manually; staged rollout available for Android apps. Since 2026-04-22 apps distributed in Russia need Russian store information or the submission is refused (notice 0000011318). Game submissions with Vietnam are refused since 2026-09-30.

### Amazon Appstore (Fire tablets)

- Status: Appstore remains on Fire tablets, Fire TV and Fire TV built-in. New app submissions for Android mobile devices stopped 2025-02-20; Appstore for Android devices and Amazon Coins ended 2025-08-20. Since 2026-11-16 the submission flow has separate release tracks for Fire OS and Vega OS. The Alexa Tablets announced in October 2026 run Android with Google Play and are served through Google Play, not the Amazon Developer Console (except Amazon Kids+ apps).
- Fire tablets and OS (https://developer.amazon.com/docs/device-specs/ft-identify-tablet-devices.html): Fire OS 8 = Android 11, API 30: Fire HD 8 (2024), Fire HD 10 (2023), Fire Max 11 (2023), Fire HD 8 (2022), Fire 7 (2022). Fire OS 7 = Android 9, API 28: Fire HD 10 (2021), Fire HD 8 (2020), Fire HD 10 (2019), Fire 7 (2019), Fire HD 8 (2018). The newest Fire tablet listed is Fire HD 8 (2024).
- Hardware: 32-bit only ABI: Fire HD 8 (2020), Fire HD 10 (2019), Fire 7 (2019); others 32/64-bit. RAM 1 GB (Fire 7 2019) to 4 GB. OpenGL ES 3.2 (Fire 7 2019: 3.1); Vulkan not stated. Resolutions 1024 x 600 to 2000 x 1200.

Up to 15 binary files per release; with several binaries devices are selected manually and each device belongs to one binary.
- Size: binary up to 2.5 GB; recommended 50 MB or less; no OBB; APK must be zip-aligned.
- Target API: minimum targetSdkVersion per Fire OS: 30 (Fire OS 8), 28 (Fire OS 7), 25 (Fire OS 6); enforced since 2022-04-01 on targetSdkVersion, not minSdkVersion. A higher target such as the Google Play target is accepted.

- 64-bit: add arm64-v8a, keep armeabi-v7a; every .so present in both folders.
- Signing: Amazon removes the developer signature and re-signs with an Amazon signature unique to the developer account, the same for all its apps; hashes (SHA-1, MD5, SHA-256) in the Developer Console. APK Signature Scheme v2+ required on Fire OS 8. Optional Amazon DRM ("Apply Amazon DRM?") on the Fire OS track when the Appstore SDK is not used; with the Appstore SDK, DRM comes from its DRM API.
- Billing: Amazon In-App Purchasing via the Appstore SDK (3.0.9, 2026-05-20). Appstore Billing Compatibility SDK: no support or maintenance since 2026-09-15. Unity IAP 5 supports only Google Play and Apple App Store; Amazon's prebuilt Unity plugin is 3.0.2 (32-bit ARM only per Unity Support); 3.0.8+ ships no Unity plugin.
- Ads: Amazon Publisher Services is Amazon's replacement for the Google Mobile Ads SDK; Amazon's Fire tablet ad SDK list (last updated 2023-08-15): AdColony 4.1.1+, Chartboost, APS, Vungle 5.3.0+, Mintegral 16.4.6+. Amazon Advertising ID with limit_ad_tracking check is mandatory.
- Push: Amazon Device Messaging or A3L Messaging.
- Login: Login with Amazon or A3L Authentication (replaces Google Sign-In). A security profile may be needed for Amazon APIs (presubmission checklist).
- Saves and game services: no Amazon service; GameCircle discontinued 2018-09-04; Google Play Games unavailable.
- Review prompt: no Amazon in-app review API found; links to other apps must open the Amazon Appstore; no rewards for reviews.
- Age verification: GetUserAgeData content provider (content://amzn_appstore/getUserAgeData); live responses since 2026-07-16 for eligible Texas users; Louisiana delayed to 2027-07-01, Utah to 2027-05-06. Significant app changes are reported in the Developer Console (since 2026-07-17).
- Forbidden in the Fire build: Google Play services-dependent SDKs; Coins references; GetUserAgeData test provider; wallpaper, launcher, lock screen or keyguard features; compensation for installs or reviews; external payment links for digital items.
- Permissions: Amazon test criteria advise against SET_WALLPAPER, CALL_PRIVILEGED, CALL_PHONE, MODIFY_PHONE_STATE, READ_PHONE_STATE, READ_SMS, RECEIVE_SMS, SEND_SMS, WRITE_SMS, ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, RECORD_AUDIO; permissions are requested in context; implied hardware features are enforced as device filters.

Privacy Labels and Content Rating are filled in the console. Live App Testing accepts APK and AAB and targets Fire OS devices; up to five test tracks; a tester role exists in the Developer Console since 2026-01-20. Alexa Tablets are not in Appstore Quality Central.

## Conflicts and open questions

1. Samsung target API: the FAQ says targetSdkVersion >= 33, the newer Seller Portal notice 0000008212 says >= 34 since 2024-11-01; this file uses 34. No Samsung notice for API 35 or 36 was found. A search result (a third-party GitHub pull request, page not reachable, unverified) claimed Galaxy Store refused targetSdk 36 binaries in August 2026. Since Google Play targets are higher, upload a test binary with the shared Google Play targetSdkVersion to Seller Portal before relying on one shared value.
2. 16 KB page size: Samsung will refuse non-compliant binaries but has postponed the date; Google Play enforces 16 KB already for its own targets. Fire OS 8 is Android 11, so 16 KB does not matter for Amazon but costs nothing.
3. Native ABIs: Google Play and Samsung require 64-bit; Amazon also requires keeping armeabi-v7a (several Fire tablets are 32-bit only). Building ARMv7 + ARM64 satisfies all three, but Amazon's prebuilt Unity plugin 3.0.2 is 32-bit only (Unity Support), so the Amazon build needs Unity IAP 4.x with the Amazon store or a custom bridge to Appstore SDK 3.0.8+.
4. Unity IAP: from 5.0.0 Unity IAP has built-in support only for Google Play and Apple App Store. The Samsung build needs the Samsung IAP Unity plugin and the Amazon build Unity IAP 4.x or an Appstore SDK bridge, so the shared purchase layer must be abstracted per store build.
5. Signing: Amazon always re-signs with its own certificate, Samsung re-signs AABs if Galaxy Store signing is used, Google Play uses Play App Signing. Each store build has a different final certificate, so Facebook key hashes, Firebase SHA fingerprints, Google Android Developer Verification entries and server-side signature checks need one entry per store. Whether Google's Android Developer Verification affects Fire OS tablets (not Google-certified devices) was not found.
6. Package names: Samsung recommends a separate package name for builds with Samsung IAP; Samsung's ADV notice then requires direct approval of that new package in Google's console (case 3). Amazon has no stated package-name rule beyond normal Android update rules.
7. Age rating: Samsung asks for an age rating during registration (self-selected; the option list was not readable, search results mention five options) and no evidence was found that Galaxy Store uses IARC. Amazon assigns a summary maturity rating from its own content questionnaire (separate ratings for Brazil); IARC use not found.
8. Age signals: each store build needs its own store API (Samsung Get Age Signals, Amazon GetUserAgeData; Google Play has its own). Neither Samsung nor Amazon states clearly whether every app must integrate; Amazon's FAQ says apps "must integrate" to receive verified age and consent data.
9. Back button: Samsung checks that Back does not force-close; Amazon says Back is optional but must not force-close and must not trap the player. Neither requires an exit dialog.
10. Ads on Fire tablets: Amazon's compatible ad SDK list dates from 2023-08-15; current mediation stacks (AppLovin MAX, LevelPlay, Unity Ads, AdMob without GMS) were not verified on Fire tablets. APS is reported by third parties to be invitation-only.
11. Amazon IAP policy says "you may not enable account creation within your app unless you use our IAP API"; how this applies to an optional game login was not clarified.
12. Unity's Android manifest: verify that the merged manifest does not set android:hardwareAccelerated="false" on the Unity activity, since Amazon test criterion 3.11 expects hardware acceleration.
13. Not verified: Galaxy Store Login requirements and Seller Portal guide pages (rendered by script, not readable), Samsung review duration, Amazon Live App Testing tester limits and IAP behavior in LAT, and whether Amazon still sells Fire tablets alongside the new Alexa Tablets (press reports say Fire tablet production stopped; no official confirmation found).
