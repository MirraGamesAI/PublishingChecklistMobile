## Build facts

Toolchain and SDK

- Since 2026-04-28: iOS and iPadOS apps uploaded to App Store Connect must be built with Xcode 26 or later and the iOS 26 / iPadOS 26 SDK.
- Announced 2026-09-09: starting April 2027 (no exact day given), iOS and iPadOS apps must be built with the iOS 27 / iPadOS 27 SDK or later. Xcode 27 shipped in September 2026; current versions are Xcode 27.2 (iOS 27.2 SDK) and Xcode 27.3 beta 2.
- Xcode host requirements: Xcode 26.0-26.3 need macOS Sequoia 15.6 or later; Xcode 26.4.1-26.6 need macOS Tahoe 26.2 or later; Xcode 27.x needs macOS Tahoe 26.6 or later.
- Upload tools: Xcode, Transporter, altool, App Store Connect API. The build must be made with Xcode 26+; the upload itself is accepted from Xcode 14+.
- Bitcode is unsupported. Frameworks that still contain bitcode fail upload validation (ITMS-90482).

Deployment target

- App Store Connect requires a deployment target of iOS 13 or later (since 2026-09-09).
- Xcode 26 and Xcode 27 support iOS deployment targets from iOS 15, so iOS 15.0 is the practical minimum.
- Unity 2022.3.72f1+ raised its own minimum to iOS 13 for UIScene support.
- Age assurance APIs need iOS 26 or later at runtime: Declared Age Range from 26.0, region signals and PermissionKit significant updates from 26.2, requiredRegulatoryFeatures from 26.4, and the "confirmed" value from 26.5.

Required project changes for the iOS 27 SDK (mandatory from April 2027)

- UIScene life cycle: without UIApplicationSceneManifest and a scene delegate, the app does not launch. Unity versions with support: 6000.0.68f1+, 6000.3.8f1+, 6000.4.0b8+, 6000.5.0a3+, 2022.3.72f1+.
- A launch screen key is required in Info.plist, otherwise upload fails with ITMS-90870.
- UIRequiresFullScreen no longer prevents resizing. The system resizes the scene discretely unless UIRequiresFullScreenIgnoredStartingWithVersion is set to 27 or earlier.
- On-Demand Resources are deprecated. Uploads containing ODR are refused from apps that never used ODR starting early 2027.
- Rebuild with Xcode 27.1+ for iPhone Duo layouts. Builds with the iOS 26 SDK run centered on its inner display.

Size limits

- iOS app: maximum 4 GB uncompressed, and the total of all __TEXT sections at most 500 MB (deployment target iOS 9.0+).
- Cellular download: the default iOS setting "Ask If Over 200 MB" prompts before downloading larger apps over cellular. Other options are "Always Allow" and "Always Ask" (third-party source).
- Apple-hosted asset packs (Background Assets): 200 GB total per app record, 200 asset packs per app record, and an email warning at 80% of the total. No per-pack limit is published. The product page "Up to" size includes the essential asset packs.
- On-Demand Resources for iOS 18+ (deprecated): app bundle 4 GB, asset pack 8 GB, 1000 asset packs, initial install and prefetched tags unlimited, in-use ODR unlimited, hosted ODR 70 GB. For targets before iOS 18: app bundle 2 GB, asset pack 512 MB, 1000 asset packs, initial and prefetched 4 GB, in use 2 GB, hosted 20 GB.
- HIG target: the initial download takes 30 minutes or less, and the game is playable when installation completes.

Signing and packaging

- Archive with Product > Archive, then distribute with the App Store Connect method. Automatic signing uses cloud-managed certificates; manual signing uses a distribution certificate and an App Store provisioning profile.
- The bundle ID and version link the build to the app and version record. The build string identifies the build uniquely.
- Entitlements to match enabled features: Game Center (required for any Game Center feature since 2023-08-16, and the features must be configured in App Store Connect), Sign in with Apple, Push Notifications (aps-environment), and In-App Purchase capability.
- UIRequiredDeviceCapabilities: list only essential items (arm64, metal). An update may keep or relax requirements, never add one.
- Device families: iPhone and iPad (2.4.1 encourages iPad support). Apple silicon Mac and Apple Vision Pro availability are on by default and are turned off per app in App Store Connect under Pricing and Availability. This applies to all versions and does not change the build.

Required or relevant frameworks

- StoreKit: use the Swift-based In-App Purchase API (StoreKit 2: Product, Transaction, AppStore.sync(), Transaction.currentEntitlements, AppTransaction, the StoreKit ageRatingCode property). Original API classes (SKPaymentQueue, SKProduct, SKPaymentTransactionObserver and others) have been deprecated since iOS 18.0.
- Review prompt: AppStore.requestReview(in:) or SwiftUI RequestReviewAction. SKStoreReviewController has been deprecated since iOS 18.0.
- GameKit: Game Center leaderboards, achievements, challenges and multiplayer. Player IDs are never displayed or shared.
- AppTrackingTransparency plus AdSupport: the IDFA is all zeros until the user authorizes tracking. The EU expanded prompt is available from iOS 27.2.
- AuthenticationServices for Sign in with Apple: revoke tokens on account deletion, and accept relay emails on the private.icloud.com domain.
- DeclaredAgeRange, PermissionKit (significant update consent) and App Store Server Notifications (RESCIND_CONSENT) for age assurance regions.
- Background Assets for downloadable content (replacement for ODR).

Info.plist keys to check

- NSUserTrackingUsageDescription (required with ATT). NSUserTrackingMarkdownUsageDescription is optional, EU only, iOS 27.2+, Markdown text.
- Purpose strings for every protected resource used, such as NSCameraUsageDescription, NSMicrophoneUsageDescription, NSPhotoLibraryUsageDescription, NSPhotoLibraryAddUsageDescription, NSLocationWhenInUseUsageDescription, NSLocalNetworkUsageDescription and NSBluetoothAlwaysUsageDescription. Each must be non-blank, under 4,000 bytes, specific and localized.
- ITSAppUsesNonExemptEncryption (Boolean): NO for no encryption or exempt-only encryption. With YES, also set ITSEncryptionExportComplianceCode. Without the key, App Store Connect asks the export compliance questions on every upload.
- A launch screen: UILaunchStoryboardName, UILaunchStoryboards, UILaunchScreen or UILaunchScreens.
- UIApplicationSceneManifest (UIScene life cycle).
- UISupportedInterfaceOrientations and UISupportedInterfaceOrientations~ipad: all four orientations on iPad for resizable scenes.
- UIRequiresFullScreen: remove it (deprecated), or set UIRequiresFullScreenIgnoredStartingWithVersion.
- UIRequiredDeviceCapabilities.
- UIBackgroundModes: only modes actually used.
- SKAdNetworkItems: Apple does not require it for review, but ad networks require it for attribution.

Privacy manifest (PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy) contents

- NSPrivacyTracking (Boolean) and NSPrivacyTrackingDomains (array). When the user has not granted ATT, requests to the listed domains fail.
- NSPrivacyCollectedDataTypes: data types collected, with linked, tracking and purpose flags.
- NSPrivacyAccessedAPITypes: dictionaries of NSPrivacyAccessedAPIType plus NSPrivacyAccessedAPITypeReasons.
- Categories and the reasons relevant to games:
  - NSPrivacyAccessedAPICategoryUserDefaults: CA92.1 (app-only defaults, for example PlayerPrefs), 1C8F.1 (App Group), C56D.1 (SDK wrapper).
  - NSPrivacyAccessedAPICategoryFileTimestamp: C617.1 (files in the app container), 0A2A.1 (SDK wrapper), DDA9.1 (display to user), 3B52.1 (user-granted files).
  - NSPrivacyAccessedAPICategorySystemBootTime: 35F9.1 (elapsed time between in-app events), 8FFB.1 (absolute timestamps for in-app events), 3D61.1 (optional bug report).
  - NSPrivacyAccessedAPICategoryDiskSpace: E174.1 (check space before writing or downloading, for example AssetBundles), 85F4.1 (display to user), 7D9E.1 (optional bug report).
  - NSPrivacyAccessedAPICategoryActiveKeyboards: 54BD.1 or 3EC4.1. Rarely needed by games.
- Unity 2021.3.35f1, 2022.3.18f1, 2023.2.7f1 and later declare CA92.1, C617.1, 0A2A.1, 35F9.1 and E174.1 for the engine automatically.
- Each SDK on Apple's list needs its own manifest and, as a binary dependency, a signature. Apple's list includes, among others: UnityFramework, Firebase (ABTesting, Auth, Core, CoreDiagnostics, CoreExtension, CoreInternal, Crashlytics, DynamicLinks, Firestore, Installations, Messaging, RemoteConfig), FBAEMKit, FBLPromises, FBSDKCoreKit, FBSDKCoreKit_Basics, FBSDKLoginKit, FBSDKShareKit, GoogleDataTransport, GoogleSignIn, GoogleUtilities, GTMAppAuth, GTMSessionFetcher, nanopb, Protobuf, Promises, OneSignal (and OneSignalCore, OneSignalExtension, OneSignalOutcomes), Abseil, BoringSSL / openssl_grpc, grpcpp, leveldb, OpenSSL, Reachability and SDWebImage.
- Google Mobile Ads, AppLovin, AppsFlyer and Adjust are not on the list, but still need manifests under the general rule.

Review process notes

- Give an active demo account, or a fully featured demo mode approved by Apple, when any feature needs sign-in. Keep backends live during review.
- Describe new features, non-obvious mechanics and every IAP (including any that cannot be reached in the build) in the review notes. Generic notes are rejected (2.3.1(a), 2.1(b)).
- Keep the App Store Connect forms aligned with the build. The App Privacy answers must cover every SDK. The age rating questionnaire must include the Social Media answers (required since September 2026) and Shared Location / Dating and Relationships (required on updates from 2026-11-16, and for all apps by 2027-01-12). Export compliance follows the Info.plist key.
- Korea: games show an additional GRAC regional rating. Apps with Frequent or Intense simulated gambling are available in Korea only with a Rating Classification Number. If GRAC issues a different rating, App Review sends a notice and the app must be resubmitted.
- Australia, Brazil and Singapore: since 2026-02-24 the App Store blocks 18+ apps for users not confirmed as adults.

## Conflicts and open questions

1. Other-platform references (conflict with Android stores). 2.3.10 bans names, icons and imagery of other mobile platforms and alternative marketplaces anywhere in the iOS app. The shared codebase must strip Android or store-specific strings and badges from the iOS build. This includes "Get it on Google Play" cross-promotion creatives, "Rate us on Google Play" texts and store-switching logic.
2. External purchase links (United States).
   - Guidelines 3.1.1(a) and 3.1.3 (2026-10-16 version) allow buttons, external links and calls to action to other purchase methods on the United States storefront without an entitlement. They remain prohibited on all other storefronts unless a StoreKit External Purchase Link Entitlement applies.
   - Commission on linked-out purchases is still in litigation. In December 2025 the Ninth Circuit allowed a reasonable fee below 27%. In April 2026 the stay was reversed and the district court was asked to set the fee. No final rate was confirmed as of the latest third-party coverage (sources 66 and 67, read through search summaries).
   - Google Play runs its own US external-links and billing-choice programs with different rules, so web-shop links need per-store and per-storefront gating.
3. Restore Purchases wording. The guideline says "should make sure you have a restore mechanism", but App Review routinely rejects apps with restorable IAP and no restore button. The checklist treats it as must.
4. UIScene deadline. TN3187 says apps built with the SDK released after iOS 26 will not launch without the UIScene life cycle. In practice this applies from the April 2027 iOS 27 SDK requirement, or earlier if the studio builds with Xcode 27 now. Unity forum reports show deep-link and URL callback regressions in some patched versions, so plug-in callbacks need testing. The minimum Unity versions come from Unity, not Apple.
5. iPad orientation and resizing. TN3192 asks for all four orientations on iPad and no UIRequiresFullScreen. Many casual games are portrait-only or landscape-only. No official source states that missing orientations cause rejection or an upload error with the iOS 27 SDK. The historical ITMS-90474 orientation error was not confirmed for current SDKs. Open: test upload behavior with Xcode 27.
6. On-Demand Resources. App Store Connect stops accepting ODR "in early 2027" for new apps or apps that never used ODR, with no exact date. Unity projects that enable ODR must migrate to Background Assets or their own CDN. Whether existing ODR users get a later cutoff is not stated.
7. Age assurance.
   - Apple's Q&A says that in regions with legal requirements "you must check the age of the people using your app" with the Declared Age Range API, but it does not list jurisdictions. The list in this file comes from Apple's news posts (Texas from 2026-06-04 after the injunction was lifted; Utah from 2026-05-06; Louisiana from 2026-07-01; Brazil).
   - Which features count as a "significant change" is the developer's decision.
   - Google Play has its own age signals API, so this is another per-store SDK.
   - Open: legal review of whether a 13+ casual game must gate content for under-18 users in these regions.
8. Age rating answers vary by region.
   - Loot boxes give 18+ in Brazil and 16+ in Australia, although the global rating is 9+.
   - Infrequent simulated gambling gives R 18+ in Australia and 13+ globally.
   - Korea needs a Rating Classification Number for Frequent or Intense simulated gambling.
   - The new Shared Location and Dating and Relationships declarations are required for all apps by 2027-01-12.
   - The page "Age ratings values and definitions" does not show the questionnaire wording or the answer options. They are visible only in App Store Connect.
9. Privacy manifests for ad SDKs. Major ad and attribution SDKs (Google Mobile Ads, AppLovin, ironSource/LevelPlay, AppsFlyer, Adjust) are not on Apple's list, so the signature requirement does not formally apply. The general manifest rule still applies. Open: verify each SDK version in the build ships PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy, and that their NSPrivacyTrackingDomains do not include domains needed for non-tracking ad serving.
10. Cellular download limit. The 200 MB default prompt is documented only by third-party sources found here. No current Apple page with the exact setting text was retrieved.
11. Encryption and France. The Info.plist key covers US export compliance. Apple notes that France separately controls the import and export of encryption apps (Secure Storage, Secure Communications, Security Anti-Virus). Typical games using only HTTPS are not in these categories, but this was not confirmed by an official source.
12. Mac and Vision Pro opt-out. Both are App Store Connect settings, not build settings, and are on by default. The help pages do not say what happens to existing Mac or Vision Pro users after opting out.
13. ATT in the EU. From iOS 27.2 the alternative prompt is mandatory in France, Germany, Italy, Poland and Romania, and the system can re-prompt only one year after the previous answer. Pre-prompt UX and opt-in rates may differ from other regions. Consent platforms (for example Google UMP for GDPR) must still be sequenced with ATT, but Apple gives no ordering rule.
14. iPhone Duo. Starting April 2027 all submissions must include iPhone Duo screenshots (store page material, out of scope here). For the build, rebuild with Xcode 27.1+ and test fold and resize behavior.
15. 3.2.2(x) wording. The current text says apps "may otherwise incentivize users to take specific actions within apps (such as by completing a level or watching an ad)", which explicitly permits rewarded ads. Rating incentives are still treated as review manipulation under 5.6.3, and Google Play also bans incentivized ratings, so both stores align.
