## Build facts

**Package format**
- New apps: Android App Bundle (AAB) only, since August 2021. Play generates and signs split APKs per device. Bundles do not support APK expansion (.obb) files; Unity content above the base module limit goes into Play Asset Delivery packs (install-time, fast-follow, on-demand).
- Partial installs of sideloaded split APKs fail on Google-certified devices and on Android 10+, so QA installs test builds through Play tracks, internal app sharing or `bundletool`, not single split APKs.

**Size limits (compressed download size)**
- Base module 500 MB; each feature module 500 MB; each asset pack 1.5 GB; install-time asset packs plus all modules 4 GB; fast-follow plus on-demand packs 30 GB cumulative; 34 GB total per app; at most 100 asset packs; apps over 1 GB need minSdk 21+; legacy APK uploads are limited to 100 MB.
- Over 200 MB, users on mobile data see a non-blocking confirmation dialog.

**API levels**
- Target: API 36 for new apps and updates since 2026-08-31 (extension until 2026-11-01, now expired). Existing apps below API 35 lose visibility to new users on newer Android versions.
- Targeting API 36 changes behavior on Android 16 devices: predictive back (no `onBackPressed`, no KEYCODE_BACK), mandatory edge-to-edge, and on large screens (sw600dp+) orientation and aspect ratio limits are ignored unless the app is a game per `android:appCategory`. The temporary resizability opt-out stops working at API 37.
- Minimum: Play sets no minSdk floor. Play Billing Library 8.1.0+ requires minSdk 23 (7.0.0 required 21). In-App Review needs Android 5.0 (API 21)+. Developer verification checks run on certified devices with Android 8 (API 26)+.

**Native code**
- 64-bit: each 32-bit ABI needs its 64-bit counterpart; Unity: IL2CPP + ARM64.
- 16 KB pages: all apps targeting API 35+ must support 16 KB pages on 64-bit devices; Play blocks non-compliant updates from 2027-02-01. Needs NDK r28+ (or linker flags), AGP 8.5.1+, 16 KB-compatible Unity (2021, 2022, Unity 6) and 16 KB-compatible prebuilt SDK libraries. Play Console App Bundle Explorer shows 16 KB status.

**Signing**
- Play App Signing: upload key RSA 2048+ in a .jks/.keystore; Google-generated app signing key RSA 4096; lost upload key can be reset; annual signing key upgrade available for installs on Android 17+.
- To share one signing key across stores: either download the Play-signed universal APK, or generate the key yourself and give Google a copy when enrolling.
- Android developer verification: package names and signing keys are registered in Play Console (automatic for about 99% of Play apps) or Android Developer Console; Android Studio Panda 4+ shows registration status when building a signed bundle or APK. ADB installs are always allowed.

**Required SDKs for the Google Play build**
- Billing: Play Billing Library 8.0.0+ (latest 9.1.0, 2026-06-18). Unity IAP 4.14.0+ bundles PBL 8.0.0, 4.15.0 bundles 8.3.0. External content links APIs need PBL 8.2.1+.
- Ads: any ad SDK compliant with Play policy; `play-services-ads` merges the AD_ID permission. Families Self-Certified Ads SDKs are needed only if children are in the target audience or ads may reach users of unknown age in a mixed-audience app.
- Login: Play Games Services v2 (`play-services-games-v2`, profile-creation suppression needs 21.0.0+) for platform authentication; Sign in with Google through Credential Manager for in-game accounts.
- Saves: Play Games Services Saved Games (3 MB per save) or own backend.
- Review prompt: Play In-App Review (Play Core 1.8.0+ or the Play Review library; Unity plugin available).
- Optional: Play Integrity API, Play In-app Updates, Play Asset Delivery Unity plugin, Play Age Signals API (beta).

**Forbidden in the Google Play build**
- Other stores' billing SDKs (unless enrolled in a Play alternative billing program for that region), self-update or APK download/install code, `REQUEST_INSTALL_PACKAGES`, downloaded executable code (dex, JAR, .so), SDK versions labeled policy-violating in Play SDK Index, Play Games Services v1 APIs (removed in `play-services-games` 25.0.0).

**Permissions and APIs to audit in the merged manifest**
- `com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID` (must match the Play Console Advertising ID declaration).
- Restricted or declared: `QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES`, `MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`, `READ_MEDIA_IMAGES`, `READ_MEDIA_VIDEO`, `USE_EXACT_ALARM`, `USE_FULL_SCREEN_INTENT`, foreground service types (Play Console declaration with video), location (minimum scope, never for ads only), Accessibility API, contacts (Contact Picker from 2027-01-27), local network (declaration for API 37+ from 2027-08-25).
- `com.android.vending.BILLING` comes with the Billing Library.

**Review process notes**
- Developer Program Policy page in force: effective 2026-10-29.
- App content declarations that must match the build: privacy policy URL, App access (demo account), Ads ("Contains ads", including house ads), Advertising ID, content rating questionnaire, target audience, Data safety (including the account deletion URL), foreground service types, permission declarations.
- Permission requests are evaluated when the bundle is added to a release. A rejected update leaves the previous version live. Removals do not immediately affect account standing, but suspensions count as strikes, and repeated or serious violations terminate the account.
- Android vitals use the last 28 days of data. Crash/ANR thresholds affect visibility and can add store listing warnings. Memory, bitmap and DEX thresholds take effect from February 2027 and may affect visibility and publishing capabilities.
- Extension forms for target API and Billing Library deadlines are on the Play Console Policy status page.

## Conflicts and open questions

1. 16 KB deadline: older announcements gave 2025-11-01 (with extensions into 2026), but the current official page names 2027-02-01 as the date after which non-compliant updates cannot be released. The page does not say whether new apps are blocked earlier. Treat the build as non-shippable without 16 KB support regardless.
2. Excessive partial wake locks: developer.android.com counts 2 hours or more in 24 hours and says it can affect visibility; the Play Console Help page counts more than 3 hours and calls the metric beta with no current visibility effect. The 5% threshold is the same in both.
3. Memory, bitmap and DEX thresholds (from February 2027) "can affect an app's visibility and publishing capabilities". Google has not said whether they will block releases, so they are listed as `should` for now.
4. Google Play Games on PC: the March 2025 blog says all mobile games are on PC by default with an opt-out, but the current submission guide describes an explicit "Opt-in to Google Play Games on PC" step. QA should check Release > Advanced settings > Form factors for each game. Chromebook (ChromeOS) availability defaults were not confirmed from an official page; apps that declare hardware features Chromebooks lack as required are not offered on Chromebooks.
5. Android 16 large-screen exemption depends on `android:appCategory="game"`. Whether the Unity version in use writes this attribute to the generated manifest was not verified; check the merged manifest.
6. Developer verification and other stores: Samsung Galaxy Store and Xiaomi GetApps are participating stores in the first phase, so their builds (same package name, possibly different signing key) must have every signing key registered. Huawei devices outside China and Amazon Fire tablets are not Google-certified, so the checks should not run there, but the same APK installed from those stores on certified devices will be checked once enforcement goes global in 2027. If a store re-signs the APK with its own key (Amazon Appstore can), that key must also be registered. Not confirmed from the Amazon side.
7. Payments versus other stores: the Google Play build must contain no Huawei IAP, Samsung IAP, RuStore, ONE Store, Xiaomi or Amazon billing code or any text steering players to those stores or to a web shop. The Metadata policy itself does not mention in-game references to other stores; the restriction comes from the Payments policy (steering to other payment methods) and the house-ad rule (cross-promotion counts as ads). Mentions of other platforms with no payment or download intent are not addressed by any Play page found.
8. US billing: Google and Epic signed a new settlement on 2026-03-04 and asked the court to enter a revised injunction; Google says more details will come. Fees and program rules (alternative billing, external content links, download fee USD 3.65 per game install) may change. The billing choice program does not cover the US.
9. India and South Korea user choice billing programs still appear on the older user choice billing page; their current status next to the 2026 billing choice program was not confirmed.
10. Advertising ID declaration mismatch and debuggable-upload rejection are documented only in third-party forum threads quoting Play Console messages; no official Play Console Help page describing them was found.
11. Unity versions with 16 KB support: the Google page names Unity 2021, 2022 and Unity 6 but links to Unity for exact patch versions, which were not fetched.
12. Account deletion conflicts with Apple: Apple requires deletion inside the app only, while Google requires both an in-app path and a web resource. Build both into the shared codebase.
13. Hot-update frameworks (for example HybridCLR or Lua/JavaScript patching) fall under the interpreter exception only if the downloaded code cannot enable policy violations; downloading DLL-equivalent code for IL2CPP interpretation was not addressed explicitly by Google.
14. Play Age Signals API (beta): Google does not require it; whether Texas SB2420 and similar state laws apply to a given game is the developer's legal call. The library version recommendation was not confirmed.
15. Localization (H) and audio (I): no Google Play rule specific to in-game localization or audio was found; only general policies apply.
